# Pairing

Pairing was a defining practice at Pivotal, but it doesn't come naturally to everyone — it's a skill with its own etiquette, failure modes, and payoffs that aren't always obvious from the outside. These articles, guides, and videos cover the mechanics (driver/navigator, when to swap), the interpersonal side (asking for help, handling friction, why it's worth the discomfort), and the case for why it's worth doing at all.

- [On Pair Programming](https://martinfowler.com/articles/on-pair-programming.html) by Birgitta Böckeler and Nina Siessegger
  - A deep dive into pair programming's practical implementation, benefits, and strategies for overcoming common challenges.
- [Pair Programming](https://agilealliance.org/glossary/pair-programming/) — Agile Alliance Glossary
  - Explains the driver/navigator model, common pitfalls, historical origins, and research findings on effectiveness.
- [Tuple's Pair Programming Guide](https://tuple.app/pair-programming-guide/)
  - Tips, tutorials, and resources for thoughtful pair programmers.
- [Simpler Machines: Pairing](https://www.simplermachines.com/tag/pairing/)
  - A collection of blog posts on pairing, including "Pairing is asking for help," "We pair because it's fun," and "Advice for pair programming that you'll hate."
- [Pair Programming: The UNEXPECTED Key to Continuous Delivery](https://youtu.be/_XBHG4AYvGM)
  - A video on how pair programming supports continuous delivery practices.
